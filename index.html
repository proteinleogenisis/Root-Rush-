Unit 5 roadmap

How to play
Runs: 3 minutes, racing a grey ghost of your best score
Question styles: multiple choice, tap the steps in order, and tap where a root lands on the number line
Lightning round: at 1:30 left (after you finish the question you're on), 20 seconds of rapid true/false for +100 each. A wrong answer locks you out for 5 seconds, so don't guess
Right answer: +100 × combo, +1 second, and up to +75 for speed
Combo: goes up every 3 in a row (max ×5)
Last 10 seconds: double points
Wrong answer: −3 seconds, combo resets, and the clock stops so you can read why
Power-ups: every 4 in a row earns one (hold 2). Freeze stops the clock for 5s. Shield saves your combo and mastery on one miss. Double makes the next 3 answers worth ×2 points. Boost makes the next 3 answers worth ×2 mastery
I don't know: tap it (top right of the question) instead of guessing. It counts as a miss (−10 mastery instead of −15), and you go straight to the step-by-step
Rematch: a type you miss comes back a few questions later. Get it right for +150
Mastery: each skill fills to 100. A right answer is +20 (+25 at combo ×3 or higher), a miss is −15. Once a skill hits 100 it stays Mastered and stops showing up in runs. When every skill in a day is mastered, take the final quiz (2 questions per skill, no timer, 80% to pass) to unlock the next day. Skills you miss on the quiz drop back to 60. Mastery run practices only the skills you haven't mastered
Quick review: after you pass a day, its skills come back every so often (2 days, then 4, 8, 16…). A right answer pushes the next review further out. A miss brings it back tomorrow
Practice this: a run with only one skill. It counts for skills and your streak, not your best score
Day streak: play once a day. Every 7 days earns a freeze that covers a missed day
Calculator and Paper: tap them during a run or lesson. Paper is scratch space for multi-step problems; your work stays until the next question. The clock keeps running
Training: if one skill is going much worse than your others (after enough tries), you'll get a page to train it after the Rush
Account: tap Sign in (top right) to save your progress and use it on your other devices
On a keyboard, keys 1–5 answer and Enter continues.
